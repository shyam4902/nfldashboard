About and privacy

Matchup Board is a free NFL and NBA site for scores, matchups, standings and stats. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

Where the numbers come from

  • ESPN's public data feeds. NBA scores, box scores, standings, leaders, rosters, injuries and player stats; NFL live scores, box scores, rosters, injuries and player stats. Your browser asks ESPN directly.
  • nflverse. NFL schedules, betting lines, play-by-play efficiency and snap counts, which Pro Football Reference box scores feed.
  • Sleeper. Weekly NFL player stats for the league leaders.
  • Win %. This comes from the betting market's moneylines with the bookmaker's margin taken out. It is the market's view, not a prediction of ours.

Every panel names its source. When a number is missing, the site leaves the space blank rather than estimating it.

Privacy

  • No accounts and no sign-in. The site never asks for your name, email or anything else.
  • Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views. It sets no cookies, does not follow you to other sites, and does not use your IP address to identify you.
  • Saved preferences. Choices like a favorite team or a table view are saved in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing your site data removes them.
  • Third parties. To show stats, your browser loads data from ESPN, team logos from ESPN's image server, fonts from Google Fonts and roster data from our Supabase database. Each of those services sees an ordinary web request from your browser.

Fine print

Matchup Board is an independent fan project. It has no connection to the NFL, the NBA, ESPN or any team, and team names and logos belong to their owners. Odds and win percentages are for information only, not betting advice.
