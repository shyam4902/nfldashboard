Matchup Board: eight directions for the NFL home

Each page is the NFL home (this week's games, standings, leaders) built on the repo's real data: Week 8 from schedule.json, standings through Week 7, Sleeper season stats and Clay's preseason projections. All eight share dark neutral chrome with the real team colours doing the work. They differ in type, density, grid and how the colour is applied. Thumbnails show each page's first screen; click one to open the live page and scroll, hover and click through it.

Design exploration on branch design/variants-2026-11. Nothing here is linked from the live site. Data snapshot is generated by design-variants/build-data.mjs from the committed JSON; team colours are the production nfl_teams values with the same contrast overrides nfl/index.html applies. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
