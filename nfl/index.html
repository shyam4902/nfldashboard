How offensive & defensive advantages are graded

The Pro Matchup Center compares teams on real measured efficiency for the 2026 season to date (nflverse EPA/play, success rate, yards per play, sack rate). Ranks are 1-32 across the league (#1 = best). Where verified data is missing, the app shows "—" rather than inventing a number.

Advantage Delta & Star Ratings Formula: |Def Rank - Off Rank|
Stars are awarded to the unit with the substantial statistical upper hand. If an offense ranks #2 against a pass defense ranked #26, the offense holds a 5-Star mismatch.

★★★★ (Delta 15–19)
★★★ (Delta 10–14)
★★ (Delta 5–9)
★ (Delta 1–4)
No Adv (Delta = 0)
